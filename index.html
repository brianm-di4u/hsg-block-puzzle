Spring Trail

Twelve puzzles named for Hot Springs places. Each has a fixed set of pieces and one goal. Solve one to collect its fact card and open the next.

How to play

Drag a tile piece from the tray onto the 9×9 bathhouse floor. Pieces never rotate. Fill a whole row, column or one of the nine 3×3 pools and it clears in a burst of steam.

Each round deals three pieces. Place all three to get three more. The game ends when none of your pieces fits. A piece that has no room is dimmed in the tray.

Scoring and heat

  • +1 for each tile you place.
  • Clearing 1 group scores 18, 2 at once 72, 3 at once 162 (18 × groups²), times the heat multiplier.
  • Every clear heats the water. A placement that clears nothing cools it a step. Heat multiplies clear points up to ×4 at 143°F, the springs' average temperature (NPS). At the top the meter reads "Boiling", after William Dunbar's 1804 name for them: "the boiling springs".
  • Empty the whole floor with a clear for a +300 clear-spring bonus.

Modes

  • Classic: endless. Score carries you through the town's seven eras.
  • Daily Soak: everyone gets the same tufa stones and the same pieces today.
  • Springs: vents sit on the floor. Clear a vent's row, column or pool to tap it (+40 × the heat multiplier). Tap 47 to win. Hot Springs is usually said to have 47 springs; one source says 43 still flow, and NPS says "about four dozen".
  • Spring Trail: hand-made puzzles with a goal and a fixed set of pieces. Undo is unlimited there.

Controls

MouseDrag a piece and drop it. Or click a piece, then click the board.
TouchDrag (the piece floats above your finger). Or tap a piece, tap the board to preview, and tap the preview to place.
